01The Problem

LitVM's entire thesis rests on one invariant — 1 zkLTC = 1 LTC

Today it's "trust us"

That 1:1 backing is asserted, not proven. There's no live, public, independently verifiable check anyone can run right now.

PoR is table stakes

After the 2022 collapses, proof-of-reserves became mandatory for exchanges. The bridge LitVM is built on needs exactly the same accountability.

If the peg ever breaks, everyone should find out instantly — not eventually.

02The Solution

A quorum of independent attestors observes both chains and posts the backing ratio on-chain.

  • Median, not trust. Health is the median of fresh readings, so no single attestor — or liar — can fake "FULLY BACKED".
  • Instant alerts. A subscribable BackingAlert fires the moment backing drops below 1:1.
  • Real, verifiable numbers. Every figure traces back to a public source anyone can re-check.
  • Public good. Zero-wallet status page that every LitVM dApp benefits from.
